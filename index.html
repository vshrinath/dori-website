Step one — capture

Getting work into Dori takes seconds.

No tagging. No filing. No "I'll write it up later." Dori meets you where the thought happens.

Say it on WhatsApp

Message Dori like a colleague. A 15-second voice note on the walk between meetings is transcribed, tagged, and linked to the right client before you're back at your desk.

Drop the file, walk away

Point Dori at Downloads, Desktop, or any folder. A client sends an 80-page report — you save it, Dori reads it, files it, and connects it to the account it belongs to.

Your calls take their own notes

Connect Fathom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams once. Every recorded meeting becomes structured notes on its own — nothing to upload, nothing to forward.

Paste a link or a video

An article worth keeping, or a 46-minute talk you will never sit through. Dori reads or watches it and keeps what was actually said, so you can ask about it later.

Photograph a bill

A restaurant bill, a hotel invoice, a taxi receipt. Dori reads the amount, the date and who it was for, and puts the line into your ledger.

Hand over a recording

An audio file from a call you took elsewhere, or a meeting nobody minuted. Dori transcribes it and files it against the people and the project it belongs to.

Everything you add lands in a project. So when you ask about that client, Dori looks only at that client’s files — a small, checkable set rather than a guess across everything you own.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this really like having an executive assistant? ▾
That's exactly how to think about it. A great EA remembers your preferences, knows your projects and the people in them, organises your chaos, and shows up ready with context when you need it. Dori does all of this — automatically, privately, and without the overhead of managing another person. The difference is Dori gets better the more you use it, works 24/7, and costs almost nothing to run.
Do I need to be technical to use Dori? ▾
Not at all. Setup takes about ten minutes. If you can use Google Docs, you can use Dori. And the help center walks through everything — from your first capture to connecting meeting sources.
Who can see my data? ▾
Your vault sits in files you own and control, on your own Mac or your own Google Drive — not in a vendor's database. Dori does not train on it and does not sell it. The one thing to be clear about: when a question genuinely needs an AI model, the relevant part of your material is sent to the provider you chose — Anthropic, OpenAI or another — and is handled under that provider's terms. Most of what Dori does never reaches a model at all, and the audit trail shows you every call that was made. If Dori disappeared tomorrow, the files are still yours, readable, and complete.
Does Dori replace my AI subscription? ▾
No — Dori makes the subscription you already have more useful. Think of it as the memory your Claude or ChatGPT is missing. Dori keeps all of your context, then sends the model only the part that actually answers the question. A session starts with the right background instead of a blank slate, and you are not paying extra API fees to get it.
What if I want to switch AI models? ▾
Just change one setting. Your entire operational memory — notes, people, threads, decisions — is stored as plain files, not inside any AI provider's systems. Switch freely, any time, and the new model starts with everything the old one knew.
How is this different from Notion AI or ChatGPT memory? ▾
Notion AI works on documents but doesn't connect conversations, voice notes, meetings, and WhatsApp messages into one memory. ChatGPT memory is limited, vendor-controlled, and disappears if you switch models or plans. Dori gives you an open, growing, structurally-linked memory that works with any model — including ones that don't exist yet.
